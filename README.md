# Doomscroll

**Turn the reels and videos you save into a searchable personal knowledge library.**

Share a link from Instagram, YouTube or other apps to Doomscroll. It lands in your Inbox instantly. In the background it becomes a knowledge card: a clear title, a short summary, the key points, steps if there are any, tags, a suggested folder, and the full transcript. Search it later by meaning, not only by keywords.

> Status: **early private preview.** Expect rough edges.

## What the preview does today

- Appears in the Android Share Sheet, saves the link in under a second, works offline
- Recognises duplicate shares of the same video
- Builds a card for each saved video: summary, key points marked as "Creator said" or "AI added", steps, tools, tags, suggested folder, transcript
- Keeps only the link, the creator details and the text. It does not store the video or the audio.

## What is coming

Folders and tags you control, search across everything you have saved, editing and correcting cards, wireless syncing, Hindi, and a much more polished interface.
